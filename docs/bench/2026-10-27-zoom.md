# Zoom and pan: main-thread cost per drawn frame (2026-10-27)

Temporary timing around `VideoView.render()` (`CLOCK_THREAD_CPUTIME_ID`), split by path: **fit** (the picture as
before zoom existed), **moving** (a gesture or animation holds the zoomed picture: one bilinear transform of the cut
piece) and **refine** (the zoomed picture at rest, scaled by its own `DisplayScaler`; MetalFX up to 4×, Lanczos past
it). Driven by synthetic events (CGEvent): ⌘= ×6 (animated), six left-drag flings with glide, right-drag zoom in and
out, ⌘0; then the video playing zoomed. Release build, a 4K H.264 video (3840×2160, readme-media
`nasa-GOES-T-launch-4k.mp4`) in a 1280×760 pt window on a Retina display (2560×1520 drawable).

Machine: Mac16,6, Apple M4 Max, macOS 27.0. Not measured on an Intel Mac (none at hand).

| path | frames | median | p95 | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| moving | 620 | 0.36 ms | 1.09 ms | 1.42 ms |
| refine (incl. playing zoomed) | 1040 | 1.20 ms | 2.19 ms | 3.76 ms |
| fit | 2400 | < 0.01 ms | 1.28 ms | 2.99 ms |

MetalFX scalers made for the zoom: 6 in the whole run, one per rest at a new zoom (a video playing at one zoom
reuses it); the fit scaler's cache is separate and is never rebuilt by zooming. All paths stay far inside a 120 Hz
frame (8.3 ms); GPU time was not measured.
